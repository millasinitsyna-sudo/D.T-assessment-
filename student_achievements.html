Pathway to University

Studying Chinese opens doors to university courses and careers in international business, diplomacy, and translation. As the second most spoken language globally, Chinese gives students an advantage when traveling overseas, offering job and educational opportunities beyond New Zealand. Furthermore, learning a language boosts cognitive thinking, problem-solving, and concentration, while deepening your understanding of foreign cultures. Many university courses recommend language studies, and proficiency in Chinese can open up scholarship opportunities both locally and abroad. Nother perk is that by adding Chinese to your resume it helps you stand out from thousands of other applicants—offering. Overall learning Chinese gives benefits for getting into university and beyond.
